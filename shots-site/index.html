Privacy and tracking, in plain terms.

Your screenshots never leave your phone. Shots has no accounts and no ads. To understand which features get used, the app sends anonymous usage events to Mixpanel, listed in full here.

Events sent to Mixpanel

App Opened. Screenshot Detected (event only, no image). Screenshot Action, with the action taken and its source. Onboarding Completed. Screen Viewed, with the screen name. Setting Changed, with the setting name and its new value.

Sent with every event

Platform (Android) and app version. Mixpanel's automatic collection is on, so its SDK may also record standard device and OS information. No name, email, or account is attached, because Shots has no accounts.

Never sent

Your screenshot images, gallery files, and file paths are never uploaded. Detection, timers, and deletion all run on your device.
